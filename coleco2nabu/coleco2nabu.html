Known titles are patched automatically from the bundled patches.z80 database by ROM CRC16 — you generally only need these switches for titles that aren't listed there.

Controls on the NABU: joystick 1 is Coleco controller 1 and joystick 2 is controller 2, so 2-player games work with two joysticks. The keyboard (keypad keys, arrows, <||| and |||>) is controller 1. SYM swaps sides (check LED on): the keyboard and joystick 1 become controller 2, for player 2's turn with a single joystick.
